Clinical interpretation aids

For care team
Trend first, not one readingReview changes over time, consistency of measurement and any co-occurring symptoms before deciding whether follow-up is needed.
Identify missing contextCheck meal timing, medication timing, recent illness, hydration, sleep and device/technique issues if a pattern appears inconsistent.
Close the loopIf the trend is clinically meaningful, document action, arrange review and ensure the patient understands the next step.
